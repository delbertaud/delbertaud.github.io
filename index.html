Automated Liquid Filling Station

Engineered for speed, consistency, and absolute repeatability, the Automated Liquid Filling Station brings professional precision to every mix. By utilizing direct weight feedback from an integrated scale directly to the central control box, the system measures every ingredient by mass rather than volume—eliminating variances caused by liquid density, temperature, or air bubbles. Dedicated dual-pump channels ensure synchronized, cross-contamination-free dispensing for both soda and liquor, topping off every glass to perfection down to the exact gram. Whether you are standardizing recipe quality or streamlining high-throughput beverage prep, this intelligent station delivers flawless, repeatable pours every single time.
